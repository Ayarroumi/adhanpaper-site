What is an adhan clock?

An adhan clock is a home display that keeps the five daily Islamic prayer times visible at a glance.

How are prayer times configured?

Use the Adhan Paper app to choose your calculation settings or match your local mosque, then sync the settings wirelessly to the frame.

Which prayers and dates are shown?

Adhan Paper displays Fajr, Dhuhr, Asr, Maghrib and Isha alongside the Hijri and Gregorian date and your selected Islamic artwork.

Why a colour e-ink display?

E-ink has a matte, paper-like appearance and no screen glow, helping Adhan Paper belong in the room like framed art.
