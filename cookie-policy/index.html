Cookie Policy

Effective date: September 4, 2026

This Cookie Policy explains how Adhan Paper uses cookies and similar technologies on adhanpaper.com.

Current Website Cookies

The current Adhan Paper website is a static public support site. We do not intentionally set non-essential cookies, advertising cookies, analytics cookies, or tracking pixels on this website.

Essential Waitlist Security

When you use the launch waitlist, we use Cloudflare Turnstile to protect the form from automated abuse. Turnstile may process technical browser and network information and use strictly necessary storage or similar technologies to provide that security check. It is not used for advertising or cross-site behavioural tracking.

Technical Logs

Website hosting and delivery providers may process standard technical information such as IP address, browser information, requested pages, timestamps, and security logs to operate, protect, and troubleshoot the website. This information is not used by Adhan Paper for advertising.

Future Changes

If we add analytics, advertising, personalization, or other non-essential cookies later, we will update this policy and request consent where required by applicable law.
